# Provenance Society marketing

Read docs/PLAYBOOK.md first.

## Voice
- Auction-house catalogue tone: quiet, precise, confident.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype words.
- Never mention prices, values or locations.
- Say "membership by invitation", never "download".

## Every piece
- Belongs to one of the five content pillars; name it.
- Instagram caption: under 120 words, headline line first.
- TikTok script: hook in the first 2 seconds, 15-30 seconds total, with shot list.
- Outreach DM: under 70 words, names one specific bottle of theirs, offers a numbered Founding Curator seat.

## Output
- Give 3 variants, labelled A/B/C.
- Add the posting day and pillar per piece.
- Save batches to docs/content/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
