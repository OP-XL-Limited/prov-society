Provenance Society — Brand & Distribution Playbook
3 Nov 2026 · @felix noble
Provenance Society launches with zero ad spend. Growth comes from 50 hand-picked Founding Curators, collector-grade content on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook, and share images that act as invitations. Claude Code uses this doc and the marketing skill at the end to draft content and outreach.
1. Positioning
• One line: A private society for serious fragrance collectors. Verified bottles, earned rank, documented provenance.
• Who it's for: Collectors with 30 or more bottles, vintage and discontinued hunters, niche-house loyalists, and fragrance creators who already have an audience.
• Not for: Casual buyers looking for recommendations. They can use the free Vault, but the brand never speaks to them.
• Against competitors: Parfumo, Prefume and FlaconKeep track collections. Provenance Society is the only one that confers status: verification, rank and provenance chains.
2. Brand voice and look
• Voice: Like an auction house catalogue. Quiet, precise, confident. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype words like "amazing" or "game-changer".
• Words we use: holding, provenance, hallmark, verified, the Vault, the Society, Curator, Maître, exhibition, acquisition.
• Words we avoid: app, users, download now, deal, discount, hack.
• Look: Same tokens as the product. Ink and Smoke backgrounds, Vellum text, Resin and Oxblood accents, editorial serif headlines, grain, bottles on dark plinths.
• Rule: Never show prices or collection values in public content. Discretion is the brand.

4. Content pillars
1. The Object: a single bottle on a plinth, its house, perfumer, year and why it matters.
2. Provenance stories: the history of a rare bottle and its chain of owners, told like an auction lot.
3. The Society: anonymised rank milestones, exhibitions, holder counts ("Only 7 members hold this").
4. Craft: perfume as art. Perfumers, materials, eras, houses.
5. Behind the build: Felix building the Society. Founder credibility for the tech and fintech audience.
Every post ends quietly with "Membership by invitation" and a link to the waitlist. It never says "download".
5. Recruiting big names
Goal: 50 Founding Curators before public launch. They seed the Society with impressive Vaults and bring their audiences.
1. List: 150 candidates. Fragrance creators on TikTok and Instagram (10k–500k followers), well-known Fragrantica and Basenotes collectors, vintage dealers, niche-house ambassadors.
2. Warm up: Follow and comment thoughtfully for 1–2 weeks before reaching out.
3. Personal invitation: A short DM naming one bottle of theirs, offering a numbered Founding Curator seat, free lifetime membership and a profile hallmark.
4. White-glove onboarding: A 20-minute call. Help them verify their first 10 bottles.
5. Ask: One post or video when they reach Curator rank, using their own words. Never scripted, never paid.
6. Track: A simple sheet: name, platform, followers, stage (contacted, replied, onboarded, posted).
Target: a 30% reply rate and 50 onboarded from 150.

7. Built-in distribution
• Every shared bottle image carries the hallmark and a waitlist or App Clip link.
• Invites are scarce, so being invited is itself worth posting about.
• Rank-up moments produce share-ready cards (no money shown).
• Year in Scent recaps every December are the biggest organic moment of the year.
8. Listening to members
The in-app feature request board (spec section 15) is also a marketing tool. Publicly shipping the top-voted request each month gives members a reason to post. A monthly "From the Society" note on Instagram shows what shipped and credits the member who suggested it, if they opt in.

Rules for any spend:
• Never run broad, untargeted ads. They cheapen an invitation-only brand.
• Never pay for fake followers, reviews or engagement.
• Measure each spend by cost per Founding Patron and cost per waitlist sign-up. Cut anything that doesn't beat organic within 30 days.
• Partnerships are always disclosed, and the creator keeps their own words.

# Provenance Society marketing

Read docs/PLAYBOOK.md first.

## Voice
- Auction-house catalogue tone: quiet, precise, confident.
- No emoji, no exclamation marks, no hype words.
- Never mention prices, values or locations.
- Say "membership by invitation", never "download".

## Every piece
- Belongs to one of the five content pillars; name it.
- Instagram caption: under 120 words, headline line first.
- TikTok script: hook in the first 2 seconds, 15-30 seconds total, with shot list.
- Outreach DM: under 70 words, names one specific bottle of theirs, offers a numbered Founding Curator seat.

## Output
- Give 3 variants, labelled A/B/C.
- Add the posting day and pillar per piece.
- Save batches to docs/content/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
12. Starter content
Founding Curator outreach

Your piece on [bottle] stayed with me. Few people talk about [house] with that care. I'm building Provenance Society, a private society for serious collectors: verified bottles, earned rank, documented provenance. I'd like to offer you one of 50 numbered Founding Curator seats. I saw your [year] [bottle]. It's a rare holding, and it deserves a proper record. Provenance Society verifies bottles and keeps their chain of ownership, privately. I'm inviting 50 Founding Curators before we open. Would you consider seat [number]?
C — Follow-up (after 5 days, once only)
A gentle follow-up on the Founding Curator seat. Seats are numbered and limited to 50. If now isn't the time, I understand completely. I'll keep admiring the collection either way.
